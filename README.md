# Nirmaana

> **Understand your money. Build your future.**

Nirmaana is a personal financial intelligence platform designed to help people understand their financial position, plan for goals, explore scenarios, and build better financial awareness.

## What We're Building

Nirmaana is focused on:

- 💰 Income and expense tracking
- 📊 Financial health and cash-flow insights
- 🎯 Goal planning and calculators
- 🔄 What-if scenario exploration
- 📚 Financial education
- 🤖 AI-powered financial explanations

### Core Principle

**Data before AI.**

Financial calculations and financial facts are generated deterministically and verified before AI is used for explanation or education.

## Product Direction

Nirmaana is being built around a simple loop:

**Understand → Diagnose → Explore → Plan → Learn → Act → Track**

The initial product focuses on helping users understand their real financial position before making financial decisions.
